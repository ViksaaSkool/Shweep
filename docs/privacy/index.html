Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SkoolDev ("Operator", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Shweep mobile application ("App").

1. Overview

Shweep is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not collect personal information, does not create user accounts, and does not transmit data to SkoolDev.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device using your platform's local storage:

  • sleep session records, including date, time, and number of sheep swiped
  • your sheep color preference (white or black)
  • whether you have completed the initial sheep color selection

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to SkoolDev. You can clear it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

3. Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase (such as the Unlimited Sheep option), the transaction is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). SkoolDev does not collect, process, or store any payment information. Purchase verification is done locally on your device through the store's own APIs.

4. Sharing

The App's Share feature sends a pre-written invitation message through your device's native sharing interface. SkoolDev does not access your contacts, read your message history, or send messages on your behalf. The content shared includes generic store links, not personal data.

5. What the App Does Not Do

The App does not:

  • collect or transmit personal information
  • create accounts or require login
  • use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location
  • collect device identifiers for profiling
  • sell or share any data with third parties
  • use background data collection

6. Backups and Sync

If your device or operating system performs automatic backups or cloud sync (e.g., iCloud or Google backup), App data may be included in those backups according to your device settings. This backup process is controlled by Apple, Google, or your device manufacturer, not by SkoolDev.

7. Communications

If you contact us at support@skooldev.com or through the GitHub repository, we will use your message solely to respond to your inquiry. We will not add your email to any mailing list without separate consent.

8. Website Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit this site, GitHub may automatically log basic server information such as your IP address, browser type, and requested pages, in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the App does not collect personal data, it does not require parental consent under COPPA, GDPR, or similar regulations.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. Session history and preferences are stored entirely on your device under your control.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through reasonable means, such as an in-app notice or an update to this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@skooldev.com.
