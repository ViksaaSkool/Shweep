4. Grayscale and Sleepstory Estimates

During a counting session, the App may gradually reduce the color (saturation) of the scene based on your recent touch activity, swipe timing, and gesture quality. This grayscale effect is a visual behavior only. It does not determine whether you are asleep and does not use motion or biometric sensors.

Sleepstory shows session information such as the date, start time, elapsed counting-session time, and number of sheep counted. "Time to sleep" and "nights" are descriptive labels based on how long you used the App, not measured sleep duration or confirmed sleep onset.

Session records and preferences are stored locally on your device and may be included in platform backups or device transfers. Completed sessions are retained without automatic expiration, and there is currently no in-app control to delete session history. You can remove App data by clearing the App's storage (Android) or deleting the App.
